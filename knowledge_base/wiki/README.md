# Wiki Layer

This is the persistent, LLM-maintained knowledge layer.

## Core Files

- `index.md`: content-oriented catalog and navigation entrypoint.
- `log.md`: append-only timeline of ingest/query/lint actions.
- `overview.md`: current synthesis and high-level thesis.

## Content Areas

- `entities/`: people, organizations, systems, places, sites.
- `concepts/`: themes, methods, processes, frameworks.
- `sources/`: source-level summaries and extracted claims.
- `analyses/`: question-driven outputs and comparisons.
- `reports/`: lint reports and periodic health checks.
